# SEA-MES: details from the data record's page

The methods text does not say who to contact about the data or under what licence they are published. These details are from the data record on the CSIRO Data Access Portal (https://doi.org/10.25919/ngqx-3557; its Dublin Core metadata is in demo/seames in the FAIRe-Galaxy repository), written in our own words for the FAIRe tools demonstration.

The record's creator is Bruce Deagle (CSIRO), with Sahan Jayasinghe, Cindy Bessey, Lev Bodrossy and Katrina West as contributors. The data come from the National Environmental Science Program (NESP) Marine and Coastal Hub Project 4.23, "Environmental DNA for measuring offshore marine biodiversity: what can DNA in water collected from the RV Investigator tell us?". The record is published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

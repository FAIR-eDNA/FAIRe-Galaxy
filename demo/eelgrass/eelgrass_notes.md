# Nova Scotia eelgrass: details not in the Dryad README

The Dryad README leaves out some details a FAIRe workbook needs. We gathered them from the sources named under each heading, for the FAIRe tools demonstration, and wrote them in our own words. They are not part of the published record.

## Primers

From the authors' QIIME 2 script (code/Bioinformatics/02_QIIME2_Pipeline.sh in https://github.com/dfo-mar-mpas/coastal-invertebrate-metabarcoding, commit 161dd75). The cutadapt step trims the forward primer GGWACWGGWTGAACWGTWTAYCCYCC and the reverse primer TAIACYTCIGGRTGICCRAARAAYCA from the reads, and discards reads without them.

## Sequencing

From the corresponding author, Nick Jeffery (by email, November 2026). The samples from 2019 were sequenced in 2020 on an Illumina MiSeq at the Pacific Biological Station (Fisheries and Oceans Canada) in Nanaimo, British Columbia. The samples from 2023 were sequenced separately, in late 2023, on an Illumina MiSeq at The Centre for Applied Genomics in Toronto.

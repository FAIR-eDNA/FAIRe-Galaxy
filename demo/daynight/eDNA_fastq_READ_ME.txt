Sample IDs on the demultiplexed and primer trimmed (unfiltered) fastq files available for download correspond to a site/time interval and replicate number.

For example:

1A_16S_Fish_R1.fastq

Here, "1" refers to eDNA site/time interval #1 (see numbers and corresponding site/time intervals below)

"A" refers to Replicate 1 of 10, "B" replicate 2 of 10 etc. There are 10 eDNA replicates per site.

"16S_Fish" refers to the metabarcoding assay. There were two metabarcoding assays applied in this study - 16S_Fish and COI_Leray.


"Filt_control" refes to the filtration control (i.e. blank) for the respective site/time interval. Filtration blanks were taken every half day (spans two sampling intervals). Therefore the Filtration control header contains two eDNA site/time interval numbers. For example, Filt_control_3_4_16S_Fish_R1.fastq refers to a filtration control taken as a control over Site/time intervals #3 and #4.


Ext_bl.fastq files refer to Extraction blanks. The extraction blank headers refer to the date in which the extraction blank was taken. For example, Extbl19_4_16S_Fish_R1.fastq indicates that the extraction blank was taken on the 19 April 2022.

## Columns written

- 'Sample ID' to samp_name (sampleMetadata)
- 'Amount filtered ' to samp_size (the number) (sampleMetadata): number taken from each cell (L), e.g. '1 L' as '1'
- 'Amount filtered ' to samp_size_unit (the unit) (sampleMetadata): unit taken from each cell (L), e.g. '1 L' as 'L'
- 'Filter size' to size_frac (sampleMetadata): number taken from each cell (µm), e.g. '0.2 micron' as '0.2'
- 'Sample processing' to recordedBy (projectMetadata)
- 'Sampling country' + 'Reef Location' + 'Sampling Location' to geo_loc_name (sampleMetadata), combined in this order, e.g. 'Australia: Great Barrier Reef, Lizard Island (Big Vicky's Reef)'
- 'Latitude' to decimalLatitude (sampleMetadata): trailing zeros (-13.99230) are shown by the cell format but dropped from the stored number, so FAIRe-fier may count fewer decimal places
- 'Longitude' to decimalLongitude (sampleMetadata): trailing zeros (-13.99230) are shown by the cell format but dropped from the stored number, so FAIRe-fier may count fewer decimal places
- 'Sampling Date' to eventDate (sampleMetadata): two-digit years read as 1969 to 2068, e.g. '12-Feb-19' as '2019-02-12'
- 'Days frozen' to samp_store_dur (sampleMetadata): numbers read as days (from the header) and written as ISO 8601 durations, e.g. '0' as 'P0D'
- kept as user-defined fields in sampleMetadata: 'EMu registration number' as EMu_registration_number, 'Exposed or Sheltered' as Exposed_or_Sheltered, 'Lunar Cycle' as Lunar_Cycle

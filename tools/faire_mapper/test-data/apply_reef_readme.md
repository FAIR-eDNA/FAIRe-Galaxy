# Lizard Island water samples (made up for the tests)

Water was collected from three reef sites in September 2017. File: apply_reef.csv

## Columns

- **Sample ID**: unique code for each filter.
- **Country**: country where the water was collected.
- **Reef**: reef system the site belongs to.
- **Site**: island nearest the sampling point.
- **Volume filtered**: volume of water filtered through each filter.
- **Pore size**: pore size of the filter membrane (µm).
- **Collected**: date the water was collected, day first.
- **Days frozen**: number of days the water was frozen before filtering.
- **Depth (m)**: depth of the water column at the site, in metres.
- **Latitude**: latitude in decimal degrees.
- **Longitude**: longitude in decimal degrees.
- **Control**: yes for a field blank (bottled water taken into the field and filtered with the samples), otherwise no.
- **Notes**: the person who filtered the sample, and any notes on the weather.
